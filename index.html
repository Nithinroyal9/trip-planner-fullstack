Traveler Resources

Key information to ensure smooth and safe travels across India.

🧳 Packing Essentials

Light breathable cottons for coastal regions; warm layered woolens for northern hill stations.

🚆 Transit & Rail

Use IRCTC for intercity train bookings and pre-paid airport/station taxis to avoid surge pricing.

💳 Payments & UPI

Digital payments (UPI) work across nearly all vendors, but keep ₹1,000–₹2,000 emergency cash handy.
